Be Prepared: The Actor's Audition Planner

NZD $34.59

You get the call. Audition tomorrow, maybe even today. Suddenly you're scrolling through old emails and digging through notebooks for character notes, losing precious prep time before you've even started. This planner keeps everything in one place — audition details, character work, scene breakdown, and notes — so you walk into every room prepared.

I know this because I lived it. I'm an actor myself, and I built this planner for me first. I needed one place to hold everything: the details, the character work, the scene breakdown, all of it. No more chaos, no more searching. Just a clear head and a focused process every time an audition landed in my inbox.

It worked so well that I couldn't keep it to myself. Fellow actors deserve to walk into every room as prepared as possible, so I turned my personal system into something I could share. This book is my way of helping you book more roles, because when one of us succeeds, it lifts the whole craft.

Each audition gets four pages: an Audition Details and Intention page so the logistics are locked in before you start creating, a Character page to dig into who you're playing, a Scene page for breaking down the material, and a Notes page left open for whatever else your process needs. The layout is kept minimal on purpose — every actor works differently, and it's built to serve your method, not replace it.

There's space for 50 auditions in total — 205 sheets, 410 pages, sized at a travel-friendly 5" x 8" so it fits in a bag alongside your sides and headshots. Cream paper, sleek minimalist cover, made to feel like something you'll actually reach for before a callback, a self-tape, or a voice over session.

This planner works for working actors, drama school students, self-tapers, and voice over artists, wherever you are in the world. It also makes a genuinely thoughtful gift for the actor in your life — something practical they'll use before every single audition.
